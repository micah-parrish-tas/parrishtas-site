About

A professional home office for associations.

Parrish TAS provides outsourced Association Manager capacity, AMC services, and executive director services. Volunteer boards focus on mission.

Your board remains the governing body. The Board owns strategy. Parrish TAS owns day-to-day ops and follow-through.

Credibility over fluff

Micah Parrish founded Parrish TAS after 25+ years in tech. The goal: give associations a sole accountable Association Manager with steady professional capacity for operations, membership systems, and the technical work that keeps a membership organization running.

Those years in systems, AMS, and channel-style operations translate directly to serving tech-adjacent and compliance-heavy associations with operational rigor.

Laura Pacini co-founded the firm with many years of experience operating an NGO overseas. She brings ops depth and coverage support under the Association Manager model. Editing and bookkeeping are specialist support under that lead. We partner remotely with clear scopes and calm communication.

How we approach the work

Clear scopes, written handoffs, and calm communication. Associations run on trust. When something needs a decision, we surface it. When something is operational, we take it off your plate. See How we work for full management, à la carte, and embedded ED.

Who we serve

We serve:

  • Trade associations (tech-adjacent and compliance-heavy first)
  • Professional societies
  • Certification boards
  • Foundations

These are organizations where volunteer presidents, board chairs, and sitting EDs need a reliable professional home office.
