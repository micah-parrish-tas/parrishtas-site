About Us

About Parrish TAS

Parrish TAS is an association management company and a member of the AMC Institute. We provide full-service and à la carte management and executive director services for associations, societies, certification boards, and foundations.

Our story

Our story

Parrish TAS was founded in 2025 by Micah Parrish and Laura Pacini. Our goal is to give volunteer-led associations reliable, professional management, with one dedicated contact and a team behind them.

Micah brings more than 25 years of experience in technology, including systems and member database work. Laura brings many years of experience running operations for a non-governmental organization overseas.

We serve clients remotely, and on-site in and near Fort Collins, Colorado.

What we do

What we do

We manage the day-to-day operations of member organizations: administration, membership, board and governance support, meetings and events, communications, and financial administration. We also provide executive director services.

Clients choose full-service management or select individual services. Optional technology and systems administration is also available.

How we work

  • Clear communication.

    We keep your board informed, respond promptly, and put responsibilities in writing.

  • Reliability.

    Deadlines are met, renewals stay on schedule, and nothing waits for the next board meeting.

  • Respect for governance.

    Your board sets direction. We bring decisions to the board and carry them out.

  • Continuity.

    Processes and records stay with your association, not with any one person.
