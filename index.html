Who we are

A management partner for volunteer-led organizations.

Parrish TAS is an association management company. We provide the experienced staff an association needs to run well, without the cost and turnover of building an in-house team.

You choose the scope, from a single service to full management. Your board stays in charge of strategy and policy. We handle the operations.

Solve common association challenges

  • Volunteers stretched thin.

    When board members handle the administration, strategy suffers. A dedicated association manager takes on daily administration, member service, and board support, with a designated backup for coverage.

  • Knowledge lost at every turnover.

    Officers change every year. Documented processes and organized records keep your association running smoothly through each transition.

  • Unclear responsibilities.

    Tasks slip when no one owns them. We agree on scope up front, so everyone knows who handles what from day one.
