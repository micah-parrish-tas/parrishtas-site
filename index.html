Staffing model

How we staff your association.

You get a primary staff lead: your Association Manager. That person is the sole accountable day-to-day contact for ops, membership, communications, governance support, and events.

The Board owns strategy. Parrish TAS owns operations and follow-through. We coordinate your bookkeeper, CPA, and counsel under board controls.

Model: one accountable Association Manager + lean specialist support (editing, bookkeeping). When the lead is onsite at an event or with another client, a named backup covers the inbox and urgent member issues.

Need a named Executive Director title on the letterhead? That is a distinct engagement path. See ED Services. Association Manager is the day-to-day job shape for full management and ED-support work. Embedded ED is the accountability and title mode when the board wants a named ED.
