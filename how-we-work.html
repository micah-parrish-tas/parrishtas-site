How we work

How a professional home office works.

An association management company (AMC) is the professional staff that runs day-to-day operations. Volunteer boards focus on mission and strategy.

For trade associations, that day-to-day job shape is often called Association Manager: sole staff for ops, membership, board support, events, and light finance with outsourced bookkeeper, CPA, and legal. Parrish TAS is that professional home office.

What is an AMC?

An AMC provides shared professional capacity under clear scopes:

  • Executive support
  • Membership
  • Finance oversight
  • Meetings and communications
  • Technology hygiene
  • Committees and vendor coordination

Your board remains the governing body. We turn decisions into follow-through.

Board owns strategy. Parrish TAS owns operations and follow-through.

Volunteer presidents, board chairs, and sitting executive directors keep mission, policy, and strategic direction. Parrish TAS owns calendars, packets, renewals, handoffs, vendor follow-up, and the operational rhythm that keeps commitments moving between meetings.

Engagement options

Full management, à la carte, or embedded ED.

Choose the depth that fits your association. Same team. Same standards. Scoped up front.

Full management

One accountable Association Manager as your professional home office: ops, membership, communications, governance support, events, and finance handoffs, plus lean specialist support. Accountable to the board.

À la carte

Boards, sitting EDs, and CEOs engage specific lanes: board packets, renewal cycles, AMS cleanup, event logistics, or vendor coordination. No full management contract required.

Embedded executive director

When the board needs a named ED title, Parrish TAS can fill that role with continuity and professional follow-through. Distinct accountability mode. See ED Services.

FAQ

Common questions from boards and EDs.

Who do you serve?
We serve trade associations (tech-adjacent and compliance-heavy), professional societies, certification boards, and foundations. Association Manager here means day-to-day staff for ops, membership, board, and events.
Who owns strategy?
The board owns strategy, policy, and fiduciary decisions. Parrish TAS owns execution of the operational work you scope with us. We surface decisions that need the board.
Can you support an existing ED?
Yes. À la carte and project support give sitting EDs and CEOs reliable capacity on membership, AMS, meetings, or vendor coordination. The ED role stays in place.
How do engagements start?
Discovery, then agreed scope, then partnership. We learn how your board, staff, and systems work. We define what Parrish TAS owns. Then we execute and report clearly. Request a proposal to begin.
