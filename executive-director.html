ED Services

Executive director services: embedded or in support of yours.

Volunteer boards need reliable executive capacity. Parrish TAS can serve as your association’s named executive director, or backstop the ED you already have. Presidents and chairs stay on mission.

Association Manager is the day-to-day job shape for full management and ED-support work. Embedded ED is the distinct accountability and title mode when the board wants a named ED.

When we are the ED

For associations that need a named executive director, Parrish TAS embeds in that role. We are accountable to the board. We own day-to-day leadership of governance ops, staff coordination, member-facing follow-through, and the calendar that moves strategy into action.

  • Clear accountability to the board
  • Continuity across volunteer terms
  • Professional home-office rhythm with lean staffing

When we support your ED

Sitting EDs and CEOs often need defined capacity. We take lanes such as membership, AMS hygiene, meetings, vendors, and board packets. Your ED stays on external relationships, strategy prep, and board partnership.

  • À la carte or project scopes
  • Written handoffs and clear ownership
  • Built for boards that already have leadership in place

Why boards hire ED capacity

Reliability, continuity, and freed volunteers.

  • Reliability

    Packets land on time. Renewals stay on track. Vendor threads get closed. Boards hire us to own follow-through.

  • Continuity

    Officer terms turn over. Institutional knowledge stays. An embedded ED or steady AMC partner keeps the machine running across transitions.

  • Freed volunteers

    Presidents and chairs return to mission, members, and strategy. We handle the operational load.
