Who We Serve

Who we serve

We work with member organizations led by volunteer boards: trade associations, professional societies, certification boards, and foundations.

Organizations we serve

  • Trade associations.

    Management for industry associations, especially technology and compliance-focused organizations. Company membership, dues, committees, and member events.

  • Professional societies.

    Support for individual-member organizations. Membership, renewals, communications, and meetings that keep members engaged.

  • Certification boards.

    Administration for certification and credentialing bodies. Board support, records, communications, and dependable day-to-day operations.

  • Foundations.

    Administrative and financial support for foundations. Board meetings, records, bookkeeping, and reporting.

A good fit

Organizations we work best with

Our clients typically are:

  • Nonprofit organizations, including 501(c)(3) and 501(c)(6) associations
  • Regional, national, or international membership organizations
  • Independent associations or organizations within a chapter structure
  • Led by volunteer boards that set strategy while staff manage operations

Is it a fit?

Is an association management company right for you?

An AMC may be the right choice if your organization:

  • Relies on volunteers to handle administrative work
  • Needs professional staff but not full-time employees
  • Has lost knowledge or momentum when officers changed
  • Has an executive director who needs more support
  • Is searching for an executive director
  • Wants one point of contact for day-to-day operations
